## What this demonstrates

**1. The emergent insight is the important part.** D1 (contribution) and D4 (unanswered
newcomers) are computed independently by our code and have no knowledge of each other. The
Mind connected them: *the community's welcoming function has degraded because the person
who performed it went quiet.* That is a systems-level claim about a community, assembled
from two unrelated signals, and it is exactly what "we compute the facts, the Mind judges"
was supposed to buy.

**2. Registry v2 worked.** The schema changes the Mind itself requested on its first cycle
— `signals.unansweredNewcomers`, `baselineReliable`, `generatedAt` — all came back used
correctly and by name.

**3. It reasoned against `generatedAt`, not the wall clock**, exactly as instructed. Had it
used "today" against a registry generated hours earlier, every figure would have been
quietly wrong.

**4. `baselineReliable` did its job.** *"None have enough posts yet for me to read a
personal rhythm — flagging the unanswered state, not a personal silence."* The distinction
between "this person has gone quiet" and "nobody answered this person" was preserved,
unprompted.

**5. The tenets held here.** *"I won't guess at causes. What you do with it is yours."* —
the perceive/act covenant, and "a gap is a question, not a conclusion." No gendered pronoun
appears anywhere in the message.

## Cost, and why autonomy was then switched off

| Time | Balance |
|---|---|
| 14:25 | 123.42 |
| 16:22 | 117.89 |

**~5.5 cognition across ~2 hours**, one observed autonomous cycle, cadence roughly hourly.

At that rate the runway is **under two days**, against a deadline eleven days out. Autonomy
was therefore **deactivated after proving it works**, to be re-enabled shortly before
filming.

This is the right trade: the behaviour is demonstrated and recorded here, and continuing to
run it produces no new information while consuming the entire budget. It is also worth
noting for the submission — an always-on Mind has a real running cost, and any honest
viability story has to account for it.
